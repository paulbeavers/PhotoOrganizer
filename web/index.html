photo organizer

Your folders. Your files. No catalog.

A fast, native Mac app for getting from a full memory card to your best shots. Copy a shoot into tidy folders by date, pick the keepers, add keywords and captions, then open the best ones in Photoshop, NX Studio or any editor you like. Everything you add is saved next to your photos, in the standard format your other photo apps already understand.

Why Photo Organizer?

Most photo organizers want to own your pictures. They import them into a library or catalog, and your ratings and keywords end up locked inside a database that only that app can read.

Photo Organizer works the other way round. Your folders are the organization. It browses the photos where they already are, and everything you add (stars, picks, labels, keywords, captions) is written into a small standard XMP file next to each photo. Photoshop, Camera Raw, Bridge and Lightroom read the same information, back-ups and sync tools carry it along, and your original photos are never changed.

It's built for working in front of the editors you already use: sort through a shoot quickly, then open the keepers in Photoshop, NX Studio or any other app with a double-click.

What's inside

Import from your card

Insert a memory card and the import window opens. Photos are copied into folders by year and day, every copy is checked against the original, and an optional second copy goes to a backup drive. Cards you've already imported are recognized.

Start culling right away

Photos appear as they're copied, so you can start picking while the import is still running. Your creator, copyright and keywords are added to every photo on the way in.

RAW + JPEG stacks

When you shoot RAW and JPEG, each pair shows as one stack you can open with a click. Each file keeps its own rating and keywords, and moving or renaming a stack keeps the pair together.

Browse your folders

Add any folders or drives to the sidebar and move through them instantly. Show a whole trip at once, subfolders included. New files appear as soon as they're saved.

Rate, pick and reject

Stars, pick and reject flags and color labels, from the keyboard, with each decision moving you on to the next photo. Then send the rejects to the Trash in one go.

Picked, ready to edit

Each day has Imported, Picked and Rejected folders. Pick a photo and it moves to Picked, with its edits, so NX Studio or Photoshop only ever shows your keepers. No second copies, and unpicking puts it back.

Review and compare

Press Space for a big, distraction-free view. Select two to four photos to see them side by side, and zoom to 100% to check focus.

Keywords and captions

Keywords with levels like Places › Iceland › Vík, a keyword list you can share with Lightroom and Bridge, and titles, captions, credits and locations.

Smart collections

Collections that fill themselves: match any or all of keyword, rating, flag, label, camera, lens, file type, capture date or import date, picked from lists of what's in your photos. Every import is kept as a collection too.

Find anything

Search names, keywords and captions, and filter by rating, label, flag or keyword. Turn a search into a smart collection, or gather favorites in your own collections.

Open in your editor

Choose a default editor and one per file type, for example NEF files in NX Studio and everything else in Photoshop. Or drag photos straight onto any app.

Move and rename safely

Move, copy and rename whole batches with templates such as date and number. Sidecars and your NX Studio, RawTherapee and DxO edits always go with their photos.

Every detail

Camera, lens and exposure at a glance, plus everything stored in the file: EXIF, GPS, IPTC, XMP and the camera maker's own notes.

Same setup on every Mac

Collections, keywords and settings are ordinary files in one folder. Keep it in iCloud Drive or Dropbox, and every Mac has the same sidebar, editors and collections. Move it, photos and all, from Settings at any time.

Where your work is kept

There is no database to back up, export or lose. It's all plain files you can see in the Finder.

Imported photosYour data folder, by date: images/2026/2026-11-19/, with Imported, Picked and Rejected folders. File names never contain spaces
Ratings, flags, labelsAn XMP sidecar next to each photo (DSC_0412.xmp), the format Adobe apps use
Keywords and captionsThe same sidecar, in the standard IPTC fields
Your photosNever modified. Raw files, JPEGs and edits stay exactly as they are
Collections, imports and settingsA folder of small files (~/Pictures/Photo Organizer, or anywhere you choose)

Install

  1. Open the download and drag Photo Organizer into Applications.
  2. Insert a memory card (or choose File → Import Photos) and import your shoot. To browse photos you already have, add their folders with Command Shift O, or drag them onto the sidebar.
  3. Choose your editors in Settings (Command ,). Photoshop and NX Studio are found automatically.

Nothing else is installed. Your imported photos, settings and collections live in ~/Pictures/Photo Organizer.
